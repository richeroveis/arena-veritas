ArenaVeritas Competitive Integrity Rulebook (v1)

All finals are settled by discrete consensus verdict bands computed on a 500 bps quantized grid over two measures: the telemetry INTEGRITY BASELINE (higher is cleaner) and the input/frame ANOMALY INDEX (higher means more cheat-like signatures).

Band definitions

  • VERIFIED_CHAMPION_PAYOUT: integrity at or above 8500 bps AND anomaly at or below 1000 bps AND no rule infraction. 100 percent of the prize pool to the verified winner, full anti-cheat bond return to both finalists.
  • SPLIT_DRAW_SETTLEMENT: a server interruption or dead heat is verified by the official telemetry page. Prize splits 50/50, full bond return to both finalists.
  • RULE_VIOLATION_PENALTY: a minor rule or settings infraction is confirmed, or the telemetry sits between clean and cheat thresholds. 75 percent of the prize to the compliant opponent, 25 percent refunded to the organizer, a minor 10 percent deduction of the violating finalist anti-cheat bond.
  • CHEAT_DISQUALIFICATION_SLASH: aimbot, macro, replay splice or other concrete cheat evidence, or an anomaly index at or above 3000 bps. The violating finalist is disqualified, the entire anti-cheat bond is slashed to the clean opponent and the protestant, and the clean opponent is awarded the prize pool.

Window rules

Verification only becomes possible after match_close_epoch. Settlement only becomes possible after protest_deadline_epoch. Unverified or uncontested brackets refund the full organizer escrow and every bond after verification_timeout_epoch.
