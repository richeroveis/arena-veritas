ARENA VERITAS settles esports and speedrun match integrity by validator consensus, not an organizer's word. An organizer escrows an AVT prize from a funded custody vault; two competitors post native anti-cheat bonds from separate wallets. Each bracket pins telemetry and rulebook pages that the leader fetches and every validator re-fetches on chain, so no self-report decides a payout. Inside the non-deterministic block integrity and anomaly snap onto a 500 bps grid and four verdict bands derive from it: champion payout, split draw, rule violation, cheat disqualification slash. Consensus demands exact agreement on all seven decision fields, no numeric tolerance near a transfer. Payouts wait for the protest deadline; every status holding money has a timeout exit. Both custody invariants are asserted inside settlement, and rejected funded calls credit a refundable balance. 82 direct tests plus 38 live transactions cover all four bands, a fail-closed LLM error and both refunds.
